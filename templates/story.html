THE STORY

FROM CONCEPTION TO GLOBAL SCALE.

A five-chapter journey outlining why BIA was created, the systemic gap we solved, the industrial technology powering our mill, our integrated synergy with Classic Fashion, and our vision for tomorrow.

01 CHAPTER 01

The Idea

Why was BIA created?

For decades, global fashion and athletic brands accepted a fundamental vulnerability: the geographic disconnect between where technical fabrics are knit and where garments are cut and sewn. Relying on fragmented international supply chains meant multi-week shipping delays, high inventory risks, and lost seasonal agility.

BIA was created to rewrite this equation. We envisioned an advanced, sovereign fabric manufacturing ecosystem in the Middle East capable of producing world-class technical knits at industrial scale, right at the doorstep of Europe and North America.

CORE GENESIS

Sovereignty & Speed

Eliminating thousands of supply chain miles by placing high-precision fabric engineering directly inside the Western trade corridor.

02 CHAPTER 02

The Opportunity

What gap in the textile/apparel value chain does BIA address?

Major apparel brands were routinely forced into compromise: choose Asian mills for technical capability but endure 45+ day transit times, or source regionally and sacrifice consistency in moisture management, 4-way stretch elasticity, and batch-to-batch color repeatability.

BIA bridges this critical gap. Located in Al-Tajamouat Industrial City, Amman, BIA couples zero-compromise European machinery standards with Jordan’s unique geopolitical advantage: the United States–Jordan Free Trade Agreement (0% import tariffs) and rapid 14-day transatlantic container shipping.

STRATEGIC ARBITRAGE

0% Tariff + 14-Day Delivery

Duty-free entry into the United States with full compliance, complete ESG transparency, and unmatched speed to market.

03 CHAPTER 03

The Mill

How BIA brings scale, technology and textile expertise together.

BIA is not an assembly of legacy equipment; it is a purpose-engineered, data-connected smart mill designed for 100+ metric tonnes daily output. We bring together world-leading German and Italian circular knitting frames operating at 35 RPM with electronic stop-motion needle monitoring, ensuring zero dropped stitches across millions of meters.

Our automated dye kitchen utilizes computerized dispensing and low-liquor ratio vessels (1:3.5) coupled with 8-chamber stenter finishing frames and an in-house accredited 4-point physical testing laboratory. Technology and master textile craftsmanship operate in total sync.

MANUFACTURING MUSCLE

100+ Tonnes Daily

High-speed circular knitting, robotic dye dispensing, continuous stenter finishing, and real-time electronic QA.

04 CHAPTER 04

The Ecosystem

Relationship with Classic Fashion.

BIA operates in deep synergy with Classic Fashion Apparel Industry — the Middle East’s largest apparel manufacturer producing over 500,000 garments daily for premier global athletic and lifestyle labels.

This integrated pipeline creates a single, uninterrupted thread: from raw yarn selection, knitting, dyeing, and chemical finishing at BIA, directly into automated cutting, sewing, and finished garment delivery at Classic Fashion. Brands gain a single accountable manufacturing partner from raw molecule to retail hanger.

VERTICAL POWER

Fabric to Fashion

Seamless handoff into 30,000+ skilled garment workers, automated cutting suites, and direct global distribution.

05 CHAPTER 05

The Future

Where BIA wants to go.

The next era of textiles will be defined by materials that do more while taking less. Through our BIA Next R&D incubator, we are pioneering mono-material circular constructions, elastane-free mechanical stretch, bio-synthetics, and 100% closed-loop industrial water recovery.

Our goal is clear: to establish BIA as the world's benchmark for high-speed, responsible textile engineering — proving that industrial scale and environmental stewardship are mutually reinforcing realities.

Pioneering zero-virgin polyester blends, mechanical stretch circularity, and complete closed-loop manufacturing.
