TRANSPARENT VALUE CHAIN

01 · MATERIAL INTEGRITY

RESPONSIBLE FIBRES & CIRCULAR FOUNDATIONS

Sustainable textiles begin at the raw polymer level. We eliminate virgin petroleum inputs through certified recycled synthetics, responsibly harvested wood pulp, and organic agricultural fibres.

MAT 01

GRS Post-Consumer Recycled PET

100% certified recycled polyester filaments derived from post-consumer plastic bottles. Independently audited under the Global Recycled Standard with unbroken chain-of-custody transaction certificates.

GRS Certified Post-Consumer
MAT 02

Closed-Loop Botanical Tencel™ & Modal

Derived from sustainably managed European beechwood forests. Processed using closed-loop solvent recovery systems that recycle over 99.8% of process solvents and water.

99.8% Solvent Recovery FSC / PEFC Certified
MAT 03

Monomaterial Zero-Elastane Stretch

Engineering 100% single-polymer polyester with helical spring mechanics, providing 4-way athletic stretch without blending elastane — allowing garments to be recycled directly back into yarn at end of life.

Mono-Material 100% Recyclable

02 · WATER STEWARDSHIP

85% CLOSED-LOOP WATER RECYCLING

Jordan is one of the world's most water-conscious nations. At BIA, our facility was engineered from the foundation to lead the Middle East in industrial water efficiency and effluent purification.

WATER 01

Multi-Stage Membrane Bioreactors

Our biological effluent treatment plant combines aerobic bacterial digestion with micro-filtration membranes, removing 99.5% of organic biological oxygen demand (BOD) and suspended solids.

MBR Filtration 99.5% BOD Removal
WATER 02

Industrial Reverse Osmosis (RO)

Treated effluent passes through high-pressure reverse osmosis membranes to remove dissolved mineral salts and trace colorants, returning pure demineralized water directly back to dye vats.

85% Water Reused RO Demineralization
WATER 03

Ultra-Low Liquor Ratio Dyeing

Our modern European Thies & Fong's dyeing vessels operate at a 1:4.2 liquor ratio, reducing freshwater consumption by over 60% compared to traditional 1:10 commercial dye houses.

1:4.2 Liquor Ratio 60% Water Saved

03 · CLEAN ENERGY & EFFICIENCY

4.2 MW ROOFTOP SOLAR GENERATION

Harnessing Jordan's abundant sunshine to power advanced circular manufacturing while optimizing thermal recovery across every drying and finishing stenter.

ENERGY 01

4.2 MW Solar Photovoltaic Roof

Over 8,000 high-efficiency tier-1 solar panels mounted across our plant roof, generating clean renewable electricity and avoiding thousands of tonnes of CO2 emissions each year.

4.2 MW Capacity Zero-Emission Power
ENERGY 02

Stenter Heat Recovery Exchangers

Air-to-water heat exchangers installed on stenter exhaust stacks recover high-temperature exhaust heat, pre-heating incoming dyehouse wash waters without burning auxiliary fuel.

Thermal Recovery 32% Thermal Savings
ENERGY 03

Variable-Frequency Drive Machinery

All knitting, pump, and ventilation motors are equipped with IE4 high-efficiency variable-frequency drives (VFD), optimizing electrical power draw in real time based on active torque.

IE4 Super Premium VFD Motor Control

04 · RESPONSIBLE CHEMICAL MANAGEMENT

100% ZDHC LEVEL 3 GREEN INPUTS

Clean chemistry is non-negotiable. We verify every dyestuff, softener, and auxiliary against strict international chemical gatekeeping lists before it enters our automated dispensing tanks.

CHEM 01

ZDHC MRSL v3.1 Level 3 Verification

All chemicals uploaded to the ZDHC Chemical Gateway with verified Level 3 laboratory certification, ensuring zero presence of banned alkylphenols, heavy metals, or hazardous azo dyes.

ZDHC Level 3 Zero Banned Toxins
CHEM 02

OEKO-TEX® Standard 100 Class 1

Every finished fabric lot is certified to Class 1 baby-safe ecological standards, guaranteeing freedom from harmful residual substances under strict saliva, sweat, and friction testing.

Class 1 Baby-Safe Tested for Harmfuls
CHEM 03

Automated Robotic Dispensing

Dye recipes and liquid auxiliaries are dispensed by computer-controlled robotic pipetting systems, eliminating human dosing error and preventing chemical overdosing or spills.

Automated Kitchen Sub-Gram Precision

05 · ZERO LANDFILL & CIRCULAR RECOVERY

100% SCRAP RECOVERY & CIRCULARITY

Fabric waste is an untapped resource. Through mechanical recycling and closed-loop sorting, 0% of our manufacturing textile remnants reach municipal landfill.

WASTE 01

100% Scrap Shredding & Re-Spinning

Selvage cuttings, machine setup leaders, and knitting test strips are sorted by polymer type, mechanically opened, shredded, and baled for secondary industrial yarn spinning.

Zero Landfill Waste Mechanical Opening
WASTE 02

Recycled Core & Packaging Loop

All finished fabric rolls are wound onto 100% recycled paper cores and wrapped in recyclable protective film, with active take-back programs established across regional garment hubs.

100% Recycled Cores Circular Packaging
WASTE 03

ETP Biological Sludge Valorization

Dewatered organic biomass sludge from our biological water treatment plant is tested non-hazardous and diverted to regional authorized agricultural composting and soil conditioning facilities.

Sludge Valorization Certified Non-Toxic
