CULTURE & ENVIRONMENT

LIFE AT BIA

A manufacturing facility shouldn't feel like an industrial relic. BIA was built as a clean, bright, dignified center of European-standard craftsmanship where human development takes center stage.

01 SKILL MASTERY

Learning at BIA

We operate the BIA Technical Training Academy in Sahab. Every operator, technician, and lab scientist undergoes structured hands-on modules in European circular knitting, digital colorimetry, and ISO inspection workflows.

  • 6-Month Apprenticeships: Machine setup, needle optics, positive yarn feeds.
  • European Specialist Training: Direct masterclasses from German & Italian technicians.
  • Continuous Upskilling: 80+ annual training hours per employee.
02 EQUAL OPPORTUNITY

Women at BIA

Textile engineering has historically been male-dominated. At BIA, women represent 40%+ of our technical laboratory, color engineering, and QA leadership teams with equal pay and clear executive progression tracks.

  • Leadership Pathways: Structured mentorship from senior plant directors.
  • Equal Pay Guarantee: Regular third-party equity audits across all roles.
  • Flexible Maternity Support: On-site nursery partnerships & phased return.
03 HUMAN HEALTH

Safety & Wellbeing

Zero shortcuts on human health. Our mill features climate-controlled air filtration systems that capture micro-lint, sound-dampened acoustic barriers, ergonomic flooring, and a dedicated 24/7 on-site medical and wellness center.

  • ISO 45001 Certified: Rigorous occupational health & safety standards.
  • 24/7 On-Site Medical: Free consultations, preventive checks, and prescription support.
  • Living Wage Standard: Exceeding national benchmarks with comprehensive health insurance.
04 CAREER MOBILITY

Growth & Development

Nobody gets stuck in a dead-end position at BIA. We maintain a transparent 5-tier technical progression ladder where floor technicians can rise to senior automation engineers, R&D specialists, and plant shift directors.

  • University Tuition Sponsorship: Supporting degrees in chemical & industrial engineering.
  • Internal-First Hiring: Over 75% of supervisory roles filled from within.
  • Global Brand Exposure: Direct collaboration with international athletic brand designers.
05 RECOGNITION

Celebrating Our People

We believe in rewarding ingenuity. Our quarterly “Master Maker” awards celebrate employees who identify yarn waste reductions, improve machine cycle efficiency, or create novel dye formulations.

  • Innovation Cash Bonuses: Direct financial rewards for floor efficiency ideas.
  • Milestone Honors: Celebrating 5, 10, and 15-year loyalty milestones.
  • Annual Family Open Day: Inviting families to experience our campus pride.
06 LOCAL IMPACT

Community & Jordan Stewardship

Our roots in Jordan run deep. We partner with local Sahab vocational schools, provide university STEM scholarships, and lead community greening initiatives to ensure our growth enriches the broader kingdom.

  • STEM University Partnerships: Internships for Jordan University of Science & Tech.
  • Vocational Scholarships: Sponsoring 50+ local youth annually in technical trades.
  • Sahab Greening Project: Planting 2,000+ native olive & acacia trees around industrial zones.
