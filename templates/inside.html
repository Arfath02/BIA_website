EMPIRICAL RIGOR · 8-STAGE CONTROL

QUALITY YOU CAN MEASURE

True quality in high-performance textiles is never accidental. Every square meter moving through our Amman facility is measured, calibrated, and validated across eight consecutive testing stages.

01 INPUT INTEGRITY

Raw Material Checks

Strict incoming yarn quarantine and lab validation before a single cone reaches a creel frame.

  • Filament Tensile & Elongation: Testing tenacity and breaking strength on calibrated single-yarn testers.
  • Uster Evenness (CVm%): Optical detection of slubs, neps, and mass variance across every spinning lot.
  • Twist & Moisture Regain: TPI verification and ASTM D2654 moisture regain equilibration.
  • Lot Segregation: 100% batch isolation preventing merge mixing or cross-lot dye disparities.
Standard: ASTM D2256 / ISO 2060
02 REAL-TIME TELEMETRY

In-Process Control

Continuous electronic monitoring during knitting, dyeing, and thermal finishing to prevent drift before it occurs.

  • Electronic Tension Sensors: Constant positive yarn feeding with ±0.5 cN micro-calibration.
  • Laser Needle Scanners: Optical defect detectors on all 120+ circular frames with instant stop triggers.
  • Automated Stenter Dwell: Sensor-monitored heat-setting temperatures (±1°C) and chamber moisture control.
  • Continuous Width & GSM: In-line ultrasonic scanners verifying continuous roll geometry.
Tolerance: ±0.5 cN / ±1°C Chamber
03 ACCREDITED FACILITY

Lab Testing

In-house climate-controlled laboratory operating ASTM, AATCC, and ISO testing protocols.

  • Diaphragm Bursting Strength: ASTM D3786 pneumatic burst testing certifying knit strength up to 450 kPa.
  • Seam Slippage & Tear: ASTM D1683 load resistance under multi-directional garment stress.
  • Elastic Recovery & Growth: ASTM D2594 testing 4-way stretch rebound and permanent deformation.
  • Conditioned Testing: 24-hour pre-conditioning at 21°C ± 1°C and 65% ± 2% relative humidity.
Standard: ASTM D3786 / ISO 13938
04 SPECTRAL PRECISION

Colour Control

Digital colorimetry eliminating human visual bias with strict Delta-E tolerances across bulk output.

  • Datacolor 1000 Spectrophotometers: Spectral reflectance measurement calibrated to Delta-E (CMC 2:1) < 0.6.
  • Multi-Illuminant Matching: Verified under D65 Daylight, TL84 Retail, CWF Fluorescent, and A Incandescent.
  • Zero Metamerism Flare: Spectral curve alignment guaranteeing consistent shade under variable retail lighting.
  • Robotic Dye Kitchen: Automated precision pipetting and chemical dosing for 100% recipe repeatability.
Target: Delta-E (CMC 2:1) < 0.6
05 DURABILITY DYNAMICS

Physical Testing

Exhaustive mechanical stress testing ensuring long-term athletic endurance and garment shape retention.

  • Martindale Abrasion & Pilling: ISO 12945-2 / ASTM D4970 running 10,000+ rubs to achieve Grade 4–5.
  • Dimensional Stability (Shrinkage): AATCC 135 wash-and-dry cycles guaranteeing length/width change < ±3%.
  • Spirality & Torque: AATCC 179 testing rotational distortion to keep side seams perfectly vertical (< 2.5%).
  • Wash Fastness & Crocking: AATCC 61 / AATCC 8 wet/dry rubbing fastness rating Grade 4.5+.
Pilling: Grade 4–5 / Shrinkage: < ±3%
06 ATHLETIC CLAIMS

Performance Testing

Empirical verification of moisture management, thermoregulation, and protective finishes.

  • Vertical Wicking Velocity: AATCC 197 testing capillary rise height (> 150mm in 10 minutes).
  • Liquid Moisture Management (MMT): AATCC 195 measuring one-way transfer index and overall moisture grade.
  • Rapid Drying Rate: AATCC 201 heated plate method certifying dry rates exceeding 1.8 mL/hr.
  • UPF 50+ Solar Block: AATCC 183 spectrophotometer UV transmittance blocking 98%+ of UVA/UVB rays.
Standard: AATCC 195 / AATCC 197 / UPF 50+
07 100% ROLL AUDIT

Final Fabric Inspection

100% of finished yardage is inspected on illuminated viewing bridges under ASTM D5430 standards.

  • ASTM D5430 4-Point System: Point penalties assigned for slubs, barre, drop stitches, and oil spots.
  • AQL 1.5 Acceptance Standard: Maximum penalty score capped at < 20 points per 100 square yards.
  • Digital Roll Defect Map: Every roll is digitally mapped so garment cut-plans can avoid any flaw zone.
  • Continuous Usable Width & Weight: In-line load cells validating GSM consistency edge-to-edge.
Standard: ASTM D5430 4-Point (AQL 1.5)
08 CHAIN OF CUSTODY

Traceability

Unbroken digital pedigree from raw polymer lot to cut garment panels on the Classic Fashion floor.

  • QR Code Roll Passports: Every roll carries a scannable encrypted QR passport linking full mill telemetry.
  • Spinning & Polymer Pedigree: Transaction certificates (TC) connecting GRS recycled or organic origin.
  • Chemical Batch Trace: Direct digital link to dyehouse recipe logs and ZDHC-approved auxiliary lots.
  • Seamless Garment Transfer: Instant scan integration into Classic Fashion automated spreading systems.
