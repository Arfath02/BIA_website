From a single filament of yarn to world-class apparel, experience how BIA’s red thread weaves possibility into industrial reality.

01 — IDEA

Performance Requirement Begins

Every breakthrough fabric begins as a target performance brief: lightweight breathability, rapid wicking, or tailored compression engineered for athletic movement.

02 — YARN

Selecting & Engineering Foundation

High-grade virgin polymers, certified recycled micro-deniers, and technical elastanes are selected and tension-tested before production.

03 — KNIT

Precision Turns Yarn into Structure

High-speed European circular knitting machines form continuous loops with electronic fault monitoring down to the millimeter.

04 — COLOUR

Dyeing Gives the Material Identity

Ultra-low liquor ratio vats and spectrophotometers ensure exact shade consistency under ΔE < 0.5 tolerances while saving 40% water.

05 — FINISH

Performance & Character Engineered

Thermal stenters and non-toxic surface treatments impart moisture wicking, cooling touch, antimicrobial resistance, and durable stretch recovery.

06 — TEST

Every Claim Has to Perform

Accredited in-house ISO testing verifies dimensional stability, wash fastness, tensile burst strength, and vertical capillary wicking rate.

07 — FABRIC

Ready for Apparel Creation

Precision-rolled technical textiles packaged and barcode-tagged with 100% digital traceability from Jordan to the cutting line.

08 — FASHION

Classic Fashion Apparel Integration

Seamless transition into garment manufacturing, precision laser cutting, automated stitching, and delivery to leading global brands.

OUR STORY

WHY BIA EXISTS

A deliberate response to the global apparel industry's need for greater agility, vertical traceability, and sustainable manufacturing scale.

The Genesis of Brilliance

BIA was founded on the fundamental principle that modern apparel brands cannot afford fragmented supply chains. Speed to market and technical performance cannot come at the expense of environmental accountability.

By establishing a purpose-built, high-capacity textile mill in Jordan, BIA unites world-class European machinery with agile manufacturing culture.

The Value Chain Opportunity

Traditional fabric sourcing suffers from disconnected handovers: raw yarn suppliers in one country, knitters in another, dyeing mills elsewhere, and final garment assembly thousands of miles away.

BIA bridges this gap with end-to-end integration, eliminating weeks of transit latency and misaligned quality standards.

The Scale of the Mill

Over 120,000 square meters of high-precision circular knitting, computerized low-liquor dyeing, and automated finishing systems operating 24/7 with over 100 tonnes daily capacity.

Strategic Partnership with Classic Fashion

In tandem with Classic Fashion—the region's largest apparel manufacturer—BIA enables complete fiber-to-finished-apparel synergy under one collaborative corporate umbrella.

The Sustainable Future

Pioneering closed-loop water recovery, rooftop solar generation, bio-synthetic yarn research, and carbon-neutral fabric developments engineered for next-generation fashion.

Think Beyond Fabric

We solve the end garment's performance, comfort, and drape challenges.

Engineer with Purpose

Every stitch, fiber blend, and finish serves a tangible functional benefit.

Precision Matters
