PERFORMANCE DATA

PROVEN IN PRODUCTION

Selected material engineering briefs delivered at commercial volume.

CASE STUDY 01 · RUNNING & MARATHON CS-PERF-01

Ultra-Lightweight Hydrophobic Marathon Jersey

CHALLENGE:

Sub-135 GSM marathon knit requiring sub-1.2s sweat absorption, zero wet-cling, and 0% US duty qualification.

SOLUTION:

32-gauge multi-channel micro-polyester with jade-mineral cooling finish and mechanical stretch.

SCALE:

45 tonnes milled, dyed, and finished with seamless cut-and-sew transition through Classic Fashion lines.

IMPACT:

28 days shaved off seasonal lead times with 100% first-pass AQL 1.5 compliance.

CASE STUDY 02 · PREMIUM ATHLEISURE CS-YOGA-02

Zero-Sheer 36G High-Density Compression Interlock

CHALLENGE:

Zero elastane grinning and opacity breakdown under deep elongation in activewear leggings.

SOLUTION:

36-gauge double interlock using micro-denier Nylon 6.6 and dope-dyed black elastane.

SCALE:

80 tonnes monthly continuous production with spectrophotometer recipe matching (Delta E < 0.5).

IMPACT:

Zero pilling after 50 wash cycles with verified ZDHC Level 3 MRSL compliance.

CASE STUDY 03 · OUTDOOR & WORKWEAR CS-TACT-03

High-Tenacity UPF 50+ DWR Bio-Proof Ottoman

CHALLENGE:

Heavy-duty abrasion resistance exceeding 60,000 Martindale cycles with PFC-free water repellency.

SOLUTION:

Structured Ottoman rib combining high-tenacity nylon with bio-based DWR finishing baths.

SCALE:

60 tonnes deployed across international athletic accounts in North America and Europe.

IMPACT:

Exceeded ASTM burst strength specs by 35% under 100% closed-loop water treatment.
