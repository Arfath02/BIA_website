OUR STORY · BRILLIANCE IN APPAREL

ENGINEERED FROM THE THREAD UP

BIA was founded to redefine the speed, precision, and sustainability of global textile manufacturing — transforming possibilities into high-performance material realities from Jordan to the world.

Positioned as an advanced vertical fabric powerhouse, BIA combines high-speed European circular knitting, low-liquor dyeing technology, automated finishing, and an in-house accredited testing laboratory. We bridge the critical gap between visionary apparel design and high-volume industrial execution.

Precision Engineering

State-of-the-art circular knitters, precision tension management, and real-time electronic fault detection across 100+ tonnes daily.

Eco-Efficiency

Ultra-low liquor ratio dyeing systems, closed-loop water treatment, and non-hazardous certified green chemistry.

Vertical Synergy

Direct alignment with Classic Fashion — seamlessly connecting yarn spinning, fabric development, and ready-to-wear apparel manufacturing.

Lab-Verified Quality

Rigorous 4-point visual inspection and continuous accredited testing for dimensional stability, colour fastness, and moisture management.

Whether collaborating with global sportswear pioneers or premium fashion labels, BIA provides rapid prototyping, dependable consistency, and certified performance at industrial scale.
