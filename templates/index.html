CASE STUDY 01 · RUNNING & MARATHON CS-PERF-01

Ultra-Lightweight Hydrophobic Marathon Jersey

CHALLENGE:

Global athletic label required a sub-135 GSM running tee with under 1.2s sweat absorption, zero skin-cling under heavy perspiration, and 0% US tariff qualification.

FABRIC SOLUTION:

BIA engineered a 32-gauge multi-channel micro-filament polyester with jade-mineral cooling finish and mechanical spring stretch (zero elastane breakdown).

PRODUCTION SCALE:

45 metric tonnes milled and dyed in Amman with seamless cut-and-sew transition through Classic Fashion lines.

RESULT:

28 days shaved off seasonal lead time, 100% first-pass AQL 1.5 audit pass rate, and full 0% duty-free US entry.

CASE STUDY 02 · PREMIUM ATHLEISURE CS-YOGA-02

Zero-Sheer 36G High-Density Compression Interlock

CHALLENGE:

Eliminate elastane grinning and opacity breakdown under deep squat elongation in premium high-waisted activewear leggings.

FABRIC SOLUTION:

36-gauge European double interlock with micro-denier Nylon 6.6 and black-dope Lycra® elastane for 360° squat-proof opacity.

PRODUCTION SCALE:

80 metric tonnes continuous monthly production with automated spectrophotometer recipe matching (Delta E <0.5).

RESULT:

Zero pilling after 50 wash cycles, buttery soft hand feel, and verified ZDHC Level 3 MRSL compliance.

CASE STUDY 03 · OUTDOOR & WORKWEAR CS-TACT-03

High-Tenacity UPF 50+ DWR Bio-Proof Ottoman

CHALLENGE:

Demanded heavy-duty abrasion resistance exceeding 60,000 Martindale cycles combined with non-fluorinated water repellency for technical outdoor outerwear.

FABRIC SOLUTION:

Structured Ottoman rib combining high-tenacity CORDURA® nylon filaments with PFC-free bio-based DWR finishing baths.

PRODUCTION SCALE:

60 metric tonnes deployed across multi-market international distribution hubs in North America and Western Europe.

RESULT:

Exceeded ASTM burst strength specs by 35% with 100% closed-loop process water treatment compliance.
