02 · FABRIC ENGINEERING

MICRO-STITCH ARCHITECTURE &
AERODYNAMIC GEOMETRIES.

We treat knit structures as engineered mechanical systems. Every needle path, loop density, and cross-sectional channel is calibrated for optimal airflow, flex-recovery, and skin tactile comfort.

ENG 01

3D Aerodynamic Textures

Engineered boundary-layer dimple and channel structures designed for elite cycling and running, reducing aerodynamic drag while maximizing moisture dissipation.

Aero Dimple Boundary Layer
ENG 02

Multi-Gauge Body Mapping

Seamlessly transitioning between high-compression supportive interlocks and open-ventilation meshes in a single continuous knitted panel without cut-and-sew seams.

Zoned Vent Seamless Transition
ENG 03

Directional Capillary Siphons

Differential yarn deniers between the inner skin-facing layer and outer face that create an osmotic pressure gradient, siphoning sweat outward instantaneously.

Differential Denier Dual-Layer Siphon

36–44 Gauge Zero Transparency

03 · PERFORMANCE TECHNOLOGIES

ENGINEERED FOR EXTREME OUTPUT.

Proprietary chemical and physical performance matrices engineered into the filament itself — delivering permanent functionality that won't wash out.

PERF 01

Dynamic Thermal Regulation

Micro-encapsulated Phase Change Materials (PCM) embedded inside yarn cross-sections that actively absorb excess skin heat when the athlete overheats, and release stored thermal energy as ambient temperatures drop.

Active PCM Microclimate Control
PERF 02

Mechanical Zero-Elastane Stretch

100% circular monomaterial polyester with helical spring geometry delivering 40%+ 4-way stretch without degrading elastane — enabling true garment-to-garment recyclability at end of life.

Elastane-Free 100% Recyclable
PERF 03

Micro-Channel Rapid Evaporation

3D cross-shaped yarn filaments delivering 2.8× faster horizontal moisture dispersion than conventional circular cross-sections, keeping skin dry during peak cardiovascular training.

2.8× Faster Drying Hydrophilic Channels
PERF 04

Permanent Bio-Silver Anti-Odor

Non-migrating silver-ion ceramic particles bound inside the core polymer, neutralizing 99.9% of odor-causing bacteria for multi-day fresh wear between laundry cycles.

99.9% Anti-Microbial Lifetime Durability
PERF 05

UPF 50+ Solar Shielding

Inorganic micro-titanium minerals blended into the spinning dope, blocking 98%+ of harmful UVA/UVB rays without chemical topical coatings that degrade in seawater or chlorine.

UPF 50+ Certified Non-Washing UV
PERF 06

PFC-Free Bio-DWR Water Repellency

Fluorine-free plant-based hydrophobic matrix engineered for running jackets and outdoor trail knits, causing rain droplets to bead and roll off without restricting fabric breathability.

PFC-Free C0 Breathable Repel
